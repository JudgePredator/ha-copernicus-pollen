# 🌸 Copernicus Pollen for Home Assistant

**Monitor European pollen levels in your smart home!**

Track 6 different pollen types plus total pollen levels with real-time forecasts from Copernicus CAMS (Europe's leading atmospheric monitoring service).

---

## ✨ What You Get

### 7 Sensors Per Location
- 🌳 **Olive Pollen** - Major Mediterranean allergen
- 🌾 **Grass Pollen** - Common spring/summer allergen  
- 🌲 **Birch Pollen** - Early spring allergen
- 🌲 **Alder Pollen** - Winter/early spring
- 🌺 **Ragweed Pollen** - Late summer/fall
- 🌿 **Mugwort Pollen** - Summer allergen
- ⚡ **Total Pollen** - Sum of all types

### Smart Features
- 🎨 **Dynamic Icons** - Change color based on danger levels
- 📊 **3-Day Forecasts** - Plan ahead for outdoor activities
- 💊 **Health Advice** - Get recommendations based on pollen levels
- 🏠 **Multiple Locations** - Monitor different cities simultaneously
- 🔊 **Alexa Ready** - Free voice announcements (via Alexa Media Player)
- 🆓 **100% Free** - No API keys or subscriptions needed

## 📥 Quick Start

1. **Install** via HACS
2. **Configure**: Settings → Devices & Services → Add Integration
3. **Enter** your location coordinates
4. **Done!** 7 sensors appear automatically

### Finding Coordinates
- Google Maps: Right-click location → Click coordinates
- Or use: [latlong.net](https://www.latlong.net/)

## 📊 Pollen Severity Scale

| Level | Grains/m³ | Icon | Action |
|-------|-----------|------|--------|
| **Very Low** | 0-10 | 🟢 | No precautions |
| **Low** | 10-30 | 🟢 | Safe for most |
| **Moderate** | 30-100 | 🟡 | Sensitive people beware |
| **High** | 100-500 | 🔴 | Limit outdoor time |
| **Very High** | 500+ | ⛔ | Stay indoors! |
